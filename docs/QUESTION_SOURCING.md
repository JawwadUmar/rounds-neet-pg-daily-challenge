# Original practice-bank research

This is the research record for the original 190 practice questions in edition `v1`, dated 18 November 2026. The subsequent supplied-paper import, its source qualifications and its verification are documented in [PDF import and verification](PDF_IMPORT.md).

## Sources assessed

- NBEMS NEET-PG 2025 Information Bulletin, section 9.7: https://nbe.edu.in/IB/IB%20NEET-PG%202025.pdf . The exam content is proprietary. Public recall compilations cannot establish official verbatim wording or authenticated answer keys. This statement does not imply that individual candidates have never received question/response access.
- Marrow's own 2024 recall announcement covers 19 subjects: https://www.marrow.com/blog/neet-pg-2024-recall-videos-release-schedule/ . Useful for syllabus/revision structure; not an official paper. No commercial recall questions are copied here.
- MedMCQA, original repository and paper: https://github.com/medmcqa/medmcqa and https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14371 . An openly available medical entrance QA dataset with broad topic coverage. Its question-level schema does not reliably establish exam year, official provenance, current guidance or the required difficulty. Wholesale import would introduce short factual questions and insufficient distractor explanations.
- MedQA, original repository: https://github.com/jind11/MedQA . A useful clinical-reasoning research dataset from medical board exams, not proof of NEET-PG appearance. Not imported or relabeled as NEET-PG PYQ.
- PrepLadder publicly available 2025 recall PDF: https://image.prepladder.com/content/NEET-PG-Recall-Questions-2025.docx.pdf . Third-party 2024 recall pages were also identified. They remain memory-based secondary exam accounts. No question was promoted to authenticated PYQ on this basis.

## Resulting bank

190 independently authored practice questions, ten in each of the 19 requested subjects. All carry `type: Practice`, `examYear: null`, explicit provenance and editorial Medium/Hard ratings. **There are zero authenticated PYQs in this release.** This is intentional provenance honesty, not an assertion that generated questions appeared in an exam. The schema supports PYQ only when primary provenance and a year can be supplied later.

Case-based localization, mechanisms, investigation interpretation and management were selected as a curriculum inference from the assessed exam resources and clinical syllabus. No quantitative claim about exam frequencies or prediction of 2027 questions is made. Basic sciences and smaller subjects receive equal representation to prevent the daily rotation excluding them. Each subject has ten different topics.

Each item supplies a single best answer, three individually explained distractors, a detailed rationale, at least two learning points and source titles/sections/links. Reference links distinguish a specific consulted guideline/chapter from a broad textbook/subject entry point; a subject index link is a reading route, not evidence that every subpage was individually audited. Text is original paraphrase and clinical teaching synthesis, not extracted question text.

## Medical checks and scope

Guideline-sensitive decisions were checked against primary guidance: NICE NG126 (ectopic pregnancy), NG240 (meningitis), WHO (rabies), FIGO/WHO (postpartum hemorrhage), ASH (HIT), KDIGO (glomerular disease), EMHG (malignant hyperthermia), ASRA (LAST) and ACR (contrast). Stable mechanisms and differential diagnoses use NCBI Bookshelf textbooks, GeneReviews, OpenStax, MSD Manual and DermNet reference pathways.

Editorial checks: sufficient discriminating findings; no missing images; physiologically consistent numeric values; plausible alternatives; no "all/none" answers; no unsupported exam years; no easy category; no repeated stem/topic within a subject; correct-answer positions distributed by a stable permutation. Automated validation verifies structure, not clinical truth.

This is an AI-authored, reference-linked educational bank. It has **not** received independent clinician or psychometric validation. Difficulty is estimated, not measured. It should undergo clinician review before being marketed as an expert-validated exam product. It does not prescribe care for real patients. References may evolve; preserve historical bank IDs and schedules when issuing corrections and document material medical changes.

## Repetition and growth

The immutable schedule uses all 190 questions exactly once in 38 days, with five distinct subjects per challenge. It then repeats with a 38-day spacing, so indefinite archives are reproducible without claiming an unlimited supply. More questions should arrive as a new date-effective bank version; changing an old schedule would break saved attempts. The current bank is a finite, balanced initial release, not a comprehensive replacement for a full NEET-PG curriculum.
